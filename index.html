LAUNCH

Six short human-verification games for queuing a limited release. Each one asks you to think about what things mean while you steer, so a bot has to understand the scene and also move like a person. Fastest time ranks highest; wrong picks add time.

  1. 1Constellations Drag the sky to find the constellation that answers the prompt, then rotate it to fit and hold it there. Search & identify · align & hold
  2. 2Ink Trail Draw one line through the objects in size order, as new ones turn up. Trace & connect
  3. 3Orbit Sort Drag orbiting objects into the basket they belong in, while the rule keeps changing. Sort & group
  4. 4Odd Lens Keep a lens on the object that doesn’t belong, as the set changes around it. Odd one out
  5. 5Night Window Shine a lamp on the objects that fit the rule as they pass the window. Timing & rhythm
  6. 6Toss & Catch Steer a net to catch only the thrown objects that fit the prompt. Spatial reasoning
