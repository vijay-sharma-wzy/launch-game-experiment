LAUNCH

Six short games for queuing a limited release. Each one has you work out an answer while you steer, so a bot has to understand what it sees and also move like a person. Fastest time ranks highest; wrong picks add time.

  1. 1Constellations Drag the sky to find the constellation that answers the prompt, then rotate the object to fit and hold it there. Search & identify · align & hold
  2. 2Trail Forks Lead a dot along a swaying trail to the one animal that answers the prompt. Route planning
  3. 3Lights Out Memorise where everything is, then scratch the cover off the thing that answers each clue. Recall
  4. 4Word Wheel Turn a wheel of words to the one that answers a written clue, and hold it steady. Read & reason
  5. 5Odd Pair Look along a row of pairs that belong together and pull apart the one that doesn’t. Compare & spot
  6. 6Two Halves Among scattered halves, join the two that make the food answering the prompt. Assemble
